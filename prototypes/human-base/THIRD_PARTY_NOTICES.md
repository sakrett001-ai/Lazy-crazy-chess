# Third-party materials

## MakeHuman Community — graphical assets, CC0 1.0 Universal

Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482

The base mesh, adult body morph targets, default skeleton, vertex weights, eye mesh, eye fitting map and eye texture are graphical data. They are covered by MakeHuman's CC0 asset declaration; no MakeHuman application code is incorporated. Historical notices identify Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.

- Declaration: `source/LICENSE.md`
- Full CC0 text: `source/LICENSE.ASSETS.md`
- Exact source files, URLs and SHA256: `source/manifest.json`

Project adaptations: adult male morph blend, declared 1.8 m control-mesh height, normalized four-influence skin, fitted training shorts, material setup, attachment helpers and deformation-study clips.
